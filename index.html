BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Can I book a specific appointment time? +

You can request a preferred date and part of the day. We'll confirm the appointment directly after reviewing the job and schedule. An appointment request is not a confirmed booking.

Do maintenance plans renew automatically? +

Not through this website preview. Plans are quoted individually, with scope, visit frequency, pricing, renewal terms, and cancellation details provided for review before you sign up or pay.

Can you coordinate larger projects? +

Tell us what you have in mind. We can discuss assessment, planning, and coordination, and determine which properly qualified trades would be required for the work.

What areas do you cover? +

We are based in Leonia, New Jersey. Availability outside Leonia and nearby communities depends on the work and schedule—send the address and we'll confirm.

Can I pay by check or cash? +

Ask about available payment arrangements when we confirm your service. Online payment and subscription checkout are planned, not yet enabled in this preview.
